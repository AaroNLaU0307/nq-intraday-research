 TopstepX™ — Commissions & Fees 

 These fees apply when trading the Trading Combine, Express Funded Account, and Live Funded Account. Commissions and fees are automatically deducted from each trade, and you&#x27;ll see a breakdown in the TopstepX platform and from your Dashboard! ​ ​ Round-Turn (RT) cost: charged when you complete both sides of a trade. Each side (Buy or Sell) incurs half the total, also called a "per side" fee.
