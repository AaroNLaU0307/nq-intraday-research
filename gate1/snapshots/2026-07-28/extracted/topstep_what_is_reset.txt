 What is a Reset?

 What is a Reset?
 Hit the limit? That&#x27;s okay. Here&#x27;s your fresh start.

 June 18, 2026 

 Table of contents

 The Basics

 A Reset returns your Trading Combine® to its original starting balance — Account Balance, Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) , Consistency Target , and trading days all go back to day one.

 When to Reset

 Hit the Maximum Loss Limit? Account becomes ineligible for funding. Reset it now — or wait for a Reset Credit at your next Rebill.

 ✌️ Reset your active Trading Combine subscription any time. Limit : 2 Resets per account each day.

 Resets and Your Rebill Date

 Resetting your account pushes your Rebill date out 30 days from the date of purchase.

 👉 Example: Rebill Aug 30 → Reset purchased (or Reset Credit applied) Aug 25 → new Rebill date Sep 25.

 Reset Credits

 Every monthly Rebill adds 1 Reset Credit to your Reset Bank. Works exactly like a purchased Reset — including the 30-day Rebill push.

 Reset Credits stay on your profile, even if you cancel the associated subscription. Available for a new Trading Combine you buy in the future.

 Rules: 

 Tied to a specific account size AND type (Standard, No Activation Fee, or DLL)

 Credits issued before 12/11/2025 — no expiration

 Credits issued on or after 12/11/2025 — expire 1 year after added

 Can&#x27;t transfer between types or sizes

 Can&#x27;t combine (two 50K credits can&#x27;t Reset a 100K)

 Can&#x27;t use credits to buy a new Trading Combine — only on an existing active subscription

 Oldest matching credit will be used first

 Topstep Pricing and Payment Questions 

 Can I Reset an Express Funded Account (XFA) or Live Funded Account (LFA)?

 No. Resets only work on active Trading Combine subscriptions. For Express Funded Accounts, use Back2Funded. Learn more → 

 Does a Reset change my Rebill date?

 Yes. Applying a Reset pushes your Rebill date out 30 days from the purchase date.

 Can I get a refund for a purchased Reset, or reverse an applied Reset Credit?

 No. Once Reset, it&#x27;s done. Always double-check the account number before confirming. If your account was in profit before the Reset and no trades were placed after, Support may adjust your new profit target.

 How do I apply a Reset?

 Dashboard → select the active Trading Combine → click Reset → confirm. Eligible credits show automatically. No matching credit? Purchase one directly.

 Is a Reset the same as Back2Funded?

 No. Resets are for active Trading Combine subscriptions. Back2Funded reactivates a lost Express Funded Account for a fee. Learn more → 

 How do I get a free Reset?

 Topstep sometimes offers free Resets on TopstepTV and through the newsletter. Subscribe to stay in the loop.
