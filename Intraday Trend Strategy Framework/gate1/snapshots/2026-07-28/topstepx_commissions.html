TopstepX™ — Commissions & Fees


These fees apply when trading the Trading Combine, Express Funded Account, and Live Funded Account. Commissions and fees are automatically deducted from each trade, and you'll see a breakdown in the TopstepX platform and from your Dashboard!
​
​Round-Turn (RT) cost: charged when you complete both sides of a trade. Each side (Buy or Sell) incurs half the total, also called a "per side" fee.

  • Minis $1.00 round turn ($0.50 per side)

  • Micros $.50 round turn ($0.25 per side)
