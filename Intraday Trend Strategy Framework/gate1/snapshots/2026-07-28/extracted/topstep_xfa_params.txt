 The Basics

 You passed the Trading Combine®. Now it&#x27;s time to trade for real Payouts. The Express Funded Account® (XFA) is the simulated funded-level account you earn after passing your Trading Combine . Your balance starts at $0 and grows from your trading profits.You can hold up to 5 active XFAs at a time. At activation, you choose your Payout path: Standard or Consistency. 

 🤜 The XFA is the proving ground. Put in the reps, prove your strategy is consistent, and earn your call-up to the big leagues. Trade with discipline, hit your Payouts, and build the track record that gets you to Live .

 Express Funded Account Standard 

 Rule 

 Do not hit your Maximum Loss Limit 

 Objectives 

 Follow the Scaling Plan — the max contracts you can hold at one time — based on your current account balance.

 Hit 5 winning days with at least $150 profit each

 If you’ve chosen to add a Daily Loss Limit, you must stay within the limits each day.

 Express Funded Account Consistency

 Rule 

 Do not hit your Maximum Loss Limit 

 Objectives 

 Follow the Scaling Plan — the max contracts you can hold at one time — based on your current account balance.

 Trade at least 3 days with at least 1 trade per day

 Keep your consistency at 40% or below: 

 Consistency = Largest Winning Day ÷ Total Net Profit 

 If you’ve chosen to add a Daily Loss Limit, you must stay within the limits each day.

 XFA Consistency FAQs

 What is XFA Consistency? 

 An alternative Payout path that rewards balanced trading. Instead of 5 winning days, you need 3 trading days. But no single day can make up more than 40% of your total profits. Consistency is reset after each Payout.

 ​ How is consistency calculated? 👉 Consistency = Largest Winning Day ÷ Total Net Profit

 Consistency tracking only appears on your dashboard once you have positive net profit. If your balance is negative, build profit first. Consistency is calculated for each payout window. Once you request a payout, it resets.

 Example: 

 Total profit $1,000, best day $400 = 40% ✅. 

 Best day $450 = 45% ❌. 

 Keep trading to bring that percentage down.

 Why does the Consistency Target exist? To reward traders who make money regularly, not just on one big day. Discipline over time beats one lucky session.

 Can I have a mix of Standard and Consistency XFA accounts? Yes, up to the 5-account limit, unless you&#x27;re on the Focused Trader Plan (FTP) .

 How does a Payout affect my Consistency? Your consistency calculation resets after each Payout and applies only to profits earned afterward. Payout eligibility must be met again from scratch.

 Example: You earn $15,000 over 3 days ($5,000 each = 33% consistency ✅) and take a $5,000 Payout. Your remaining balance is $10,000, but your consistency clock resets. You now need 3 new qualifying days on profits earned after that Payout.

 Express Funded Account FAQs

 Can I have more than 1 XFA?

 Yes, up to 5 active at a time. If you pass a Trading Combine® while at the limit, the new XFA goes on hold until one closes. Shoulder Tap account holders are limited to 1 XFA.

 What happens if I breach the Maximum Loss Limit?

 Your account is liquidated and closed at the end of the day. Back2Funded lets you reactivate up to 2 times at the same size and Payout policy.

 Can I mix account sizes?

 Yes. Example: two 50K XFAs and three 150K XFAs. 

 Can I change my XFA size or type?

 No. Your size matches the Trading Combine® you passed and is locked before and after activation. Once you select XFA Consistency or Standard, that choice cannot be changed either.

 Why does my XFA show a $0 balance?

 This is expected. Your buying power is still the full account size (50K, 100K, or 150K). Your balance grows as you earn profits.

 What are the fees?

 No monthly subscription fee after passing. A one-time Activation Fee applies per XFA (Unless you chose the No Activation Fee Trading Combine). See Topstep Pricing for current rates.

 Do I need a data subscription?

 No. Level 1 (Top of Book) data is included with your Topstep membership. Level 2 (Depth of Market) is available as an upgrade. See Level 1 and Level 2 Market Data .

 How long does it take to get my XFA?

 Minutes after paying the Activation Fee . If you pass on a Friday, your XFA won&#x27;t be available until markets reopen at 5 PM CT Sunday.

 What happens if I go inactive?

 XFAs with no trading activity for more than 30 days may be closed. XFAs can&#x27;t be put on hold.

 How do Payouts work on a simulated account?

 Topstep pays you real money based on your simulated trading results. See the Payout Policy for how to qualify and request.

 Can I get called up to a Live Funded Account?

 That&#x27;s the goal — the call-up. The Risk Team is watching your tape. Keep trading consistently and let your performance make the case. Learn more here .

 Can I use automated strategies?

 Yes, with conditions. Topstep won&#x27;t help set them up or cover malfunctions. Review Prohibited Conduct and Prohibited Trading Strategies first.

 Can I trade across multiple XFAs at once?

 Yes, up to $750K buying power using a trade copier. See What is a Trade Copier . Hedging across accounts and coordinated trading with others is prohibited. See Understanding Hedging and Topstep&#x27;s Terms of Use .
