 LucidFlex Funded Account Scaling Plan

 The scaling plan controls how much of your max size is available to trade based on the simulated profits in your account. As your simulated profits in the funded account increase and decrease, your max tradable contract size moves up and down based. There is no scaling plan in the evaluation phase. ​ The scaling plan updates at the end of each session, so your limits will not update in real-time throughout the day.  Circumventing the Scaling Plan

 Some trader may be inclined to try and get around the scaling plan. Our back-end systems track and adjust balances for this. If we see repeated behavior attempting to get around the limits, we may review your account.

 What is a Scaling Plan for a Futures Prop Firm Account?

 A scaling plan in futures trading controls how many contracts you&#x27;re allowed to trade at one time in your funded account. Your limit is tied directly to your simulated profit balance. The more you&#x27;ve earned, the more buying power you have access to. Unlike many prop firms, LucidFlex has no scaling restrictions during the evaluation phase. You have access to your full max contract size from your first trade.

 Scaling Plan FAQs

 Does the scaling plan apply during my evaluation? 

 No. There is no scaling plan in the LucidFlex evaluation phase.

 When does my contract limit update? 

 At the end of each trading session. It does not update in real-time during the day.

 What happens to my contract limit if I take a payout? 

 Since payouts reduce your simulated balance, your scaling tier may adjust downward to reflect the updated profit level.

 Can I trade my full contract size from day one in the funded account? 

 Your starting size is determined by your initial profit level per the scaling table. As you build profits, your max size grows.

 What if I accidentally exceed my contract limit? 

 Each platform/datafeed provider has guardrails in place to prevent/block this a much as possible, however users whom intentionally try to, or succeed in circumventing these limits may result in having profit wiped for the day.
