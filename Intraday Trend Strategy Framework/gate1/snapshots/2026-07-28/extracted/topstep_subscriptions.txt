 Trading Combine Subscriptions

 Trading Combine Subscriptions
 Auto-renews monthly and banks a Reset Credit with every Rebill. Here&#x27;s what you need to know.

 June 18, 2026 

 Table of contents

 The Basics

 The Trading Combine® is a monthly subscription. It Rebills every 30 days from your original sign-up date. Active until you pass and earn an Express Funded Account® (XFA) — or until you cancel. No time limit for passing.

 Check your Rebill date and manage your subscription from your Topstep Dashboard .

 Trading Combine Subscription FAQs

 What does the Trading Combine cost?

 Topstep Pricing and Payment Information → 

 What payment methods are accepted?

 Credit or debit card: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.

 👉 Must be in your own name — using someone else&#x27;s card violates Topstep&#x27;s Terms of Use.

 Important: Prepaid cards and gift cards (including Cash App and Venmo) may not work.

 How do I cancel?

 Billing page → click " X " next to the subscription → confirm .

 Once canceled, it cannot be undone. No resubscribe option. You also can&#x27;t purchase a Reset on that subscription after it&#x27;s canceled.

 What happens if I break a rule (hit the Maximum Loss Limit)?

 Your account becomes ineligible for funding until it&#x27;s reset. The subscription does not cancel — it keeps rebilling, and you can still trade for practice. You&#x27;ll get a Reset Credit when your Rebill processes. 

 Can I pause my Trading Combine?

 No. Subscriptions can&#x27;t be paused or put on hold. It will Rebill monthly until you pass or cancel.

 When do I need to pass to avoid being Rebilled?

 Your Dashboard must show you&#x27;ve passed before your Rebill date. 

 Example: Rebill is the 5th → pass before trading ends on the 4th.

 What happens to my subscription after I pass?

 The subscription tied to the passed Trading Combine will auto-cancel, and the account will close. You&#x27;ll be able to activate your Express Funded Account immediately. Other active Trading Combines keep Rebilling until you manually cancel them.

 I passed, but I&#x27;m still being charged, what should I do?

 Check your Billing page for any other active Trading Combine subscriptions that haven&#x27;t been passed yet. These will continue to rebill automatically until you cancel them manually. If you believe you&#x27;re being charged in error for the passed account specifically, please contact support.

 Why did I receive a cancellation email after I passed? 

 When your Trading Combine is marked as Passed, the subscription for that account is automatically canceled. The cancellation email you receive is a standard notification confirming this — it does not indicate any problem with your account or your progress toward funding.

 What happens to my Reset Credits if I cancel?

 Reset Credits stay on your profile. Available for any future Trading Combine (of the same size/type). However, once a subscription is canceled, you can&#x27;t Reset that specific account anymore.

 Can I reactivate a canceled subscription?

 No. Once canceled, it&#x27;s gone. Start a new Trading Combine to keep trading.

 Can I have more than one Trading Combine at a time?

 Yes — no limit on how many you can have at once. ​ ​ Pro tip: Always confirm which account you&#x27;re in before placing trades.
