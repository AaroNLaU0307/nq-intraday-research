 The Basics

 Real capital. Real markets. The Live Funded Account® (LFA) is the big leagues — Topstep&#x27;s money behind you, no Payout caps, and unlimited growth potential. When the call-up comes, all Express Funded Accounts (XFA) close. This is what you&#x27;ve been working toward.

 Live Account Size

 Your LFA size is based on the average of all active, eligible XFAs with at least 1 Payout. If you haven&#x27;t requested any Payouts yet, all XFAs are averaged together. The average is rounded up to the nearest tier ($50K, $100K, or $150K).

 Example: Four 50K XFAs + one 150K XFA = 70K average → rounds up to $100K LFA .

 Starting Balance and Reserve

 At Live activation:

 20% of combined XFA balances is available for trading immediately

 Minimum starting balance: $10,000 (Topstep supplements from Reserve if needed)

 80% held in Reserve — unlocked in 4 increments as you hit profit thresholds

 Each threshold = 25% of Reserve released

 Example: $75K combined profit → $15K to trade, $60K in Reserve.

 Starting balance is capped at your account size. Can&#x27;t transfer more than $100K into a $100K LFA. The excess is forfeited. 

 👉 Heads up: all Express Funded Accounts are averaged together, so new Topstep Labs accounts may affect your account size.

 Capital Expansion — Unlocking Your Reserve

 Reserve funds unlock in 25% increments each time you hit the Profit Target for your LFA size. The Profit Target mirrors your Trading Combine® profit target:

 Capital Expansion is: 

 Reviewed every Monday morning 

 Funds deposited within 1-2 business days

 Can be delayed or denied for excessive or reckless risk behavior

 You cannot unlock multiple tiers with a single large win — each threshold requires net profit since the last expansion .

 Important: If your LFA balance drops below $1,000 , the account may be immediately liquidated and closed at end of the trading day. The remaining balance would then be sent as a final Payout. ​ ​ Example: Account drops to $800 = auto liquidation and account closure at the end of that day. The remaining $800 is sent as a final Payout. Unlocked reserve is forfeited.

 Live Funded Account FAQs

 Can I decline the invitation to move to Live and stay in my XFA?

 No. Once the Risk Team determines you&#x27;re ready, your options are to move to Live or close your Express Funded Account.

 How long does it take to create a Live Funded Account?

 7 to 10 business days. Topstep will notify you as soon as it&#x27;s ready.

 How is my Live Funded Account size determined if I haven&#x27;t received any Payouts yet?

 In most cases, only Express Funded Accounts with at least 1 Payout are used to calculate your LFA size. However, if you haven&#x27;t received any Payouts at the time you&#x27;re called up, all of your active, eligible Express Funded Accounts will be averaged together instead.

 Can I have more than one Live Funded Account?

 You can only have one (1) Live Funded Account active. When you receive a Live Funded Account, all Express Funded Accounts are closed.

 If I lose my LFA, can I go straight back to an XFA without a Trading Combine?

 No. You must pass a Trading Combine first. From there, demonstrate consistency in an XFA before being called back up to Live. 

 Can I take Payouts before unlocking all my Reserve?

 Yes. Payout eligibility is not tied to capital expansion. You can take Payouts while still trading with partial account access.

 How do I request a Payout?

 👉 Check out our Payout Policy for steps

 Is the live trading capital my money?

 No. It&#x27;s Topstep&#x27;s prop firm capital made available as you demonstrate consistent performance. Once you withdraw funds via a Payout, those funds are yours.

 Can I unlock multiple Reserve tiers at once with a big win?

 No. Each tier requires net profit since the last expansion, reviewed weekly. Single oversized wins do not bypass this.

 Can I use automated strategies in my LFA?

 Automated strategies are permitted, but Topstep cannot help set them up or troubleshoot them. Automated trading via the ProjectX API is prohibited in the LFA.

 How does trading Live benefit me over SIM?

 Everything counts here. Real capital, real consequences, real payouts with no cap. The XFA proved your strategy works — Live is where you get paid like it. Trading Live is the goal. It means managing real money, which helps develop discipline and skills under real market conditions, preparing you for real-world trading at Topstep and beyond.

 Once I have 30 winning days, can I take a 100% payout?

 Yes. We encourage Traders to take full advantage of the benefits of Live trading with Topstep, including unlimited growth potential and daily Payouts. Please note that Payouts can only be taken from your unlocked balance, not from your Live Funded Account Reserve.​ Please note: withdrawing the full balance will close your account.

 As you reach each profit target, additional funds are released from your Reserve into your unlocked balance. Once 100% of your balance has been unlocked, you may withdraw those funds as well. 

 What happens if I lose my 20% starting balance? 

 If your Live Funded Account balance drops below $1,000, the account will be liquidated immediately and closed at the end of the trading day. The remaining balance will be sent to you as a final payout before the account is closed. ​ ​ Example: Account drops to $800 = auto liquidation and account closure at the end of that day. The remaining $800 is sent as a final Payout. Unlocked reserve is forfeited.
