**Refuter lenses.**
- **threat-path** walks the stated failure path from the actual data and state. In its pure form it takes the finder's evidence as true.
- **rule-text** tests the claim against the binding text in scope.
- **reproduce-from-bytes** re-derives the evidence from committed bytes.

Two kinds of vote used the pure threat-path lens instead: the cached single votes (rule single-1-cached) and the third vote on R2-G11 (rule complete-3).

**Survival rules.** These were applied exactly, and the synthesis changed no outcome.
- **decided-3:** three cached votes on the representative finding. CONFIRMED if at least 2 of 3 did not refute.
- **complete-3:** two cached votes plus the continuation threat-path vote. CONFIRMED if at least 2 of 3 did not refute.
- **single-1-cached:** one cached pure threat-path vote decides.
- **single-1:** one continuation sole threat-path vote, with evidence check, decides. CONFIRMED if not refuted.
- **Critic findings:** Critical or High would get three votes under the 2-of-3 rule; Medium or Low get one sole threat-path vote. R2-critic-1 is Low.
- **Final severity** is at most the highest non-refuting severity_view among the deciding votes.
- **Narrowed groups** are confirmed only as narrowed.
- **Merged members' sub-claims** that no vote examined are marked "raised, not separately verified".
