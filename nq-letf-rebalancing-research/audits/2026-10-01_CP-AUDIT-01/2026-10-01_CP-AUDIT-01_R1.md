**Refuter lenses.**
- *reproduce-from-bytes* re-derives the evidence from the bytes and tries to refute it.
- *rule-text* reads the binding text that was in force at the time, in scope.
- *threat-path* assumes the evidence is true and follows the failure path, asking whether it reaches a wrong conclusion, a false record or an unverifiable record.

A finding survives a vote that does not refute it. The outcome rules were applied exactly:
- **decided-3**: three cached votes on the representative decide. CONFIRMED iff at least 2 of 3 did not refute.
- **complete-3**: two cached votes plus one continuation threat-path vote, under the same 2-of-3 rule.
- **single-1-cached**: one cached pure threat-path vote decides. No R1 group used this rule.
- **single-1**: one continuation sole threat-path vote decides; that vote also checked the evidence against the bytes. CONFIRMED iff not refuted.
- **critic findings**: a Critical or High finding would get three votes under the 2-of-3 rule; Medium and Low get one sole threat-path vote. All four R1 critic findings were filed Low.

A vote that narrowed a finding confirms only the part that stands. Final severity is at most the highest non-refuting severity view among the deciding votes. A finding is labelled REPRODUCED only if the finder, or a non-refuting deciding vote, ran or recomputed it and observed the result.
